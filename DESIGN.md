# Admin College App Design System

## Typography
*   **Font Family:** Inter
*   **Headings:** Bold, primary color
*   **Body:** Regular, dark grey

## Colors
*   **Primary:** Deep Blue (#0277bd)
*   **Background (Light):** Soft Cyan (#E3F4F4)
*   **Card Background:** White (#FFFFFF)
*   **Text:** Dark Grey (#121212)
*   **Accent Colors:**
    *   Purple: #713ABE (Gallery)
    *   Yellow: #FFD23F (Faculty)
    *   Green: #36AE7C (Notice)
    *   Red: #EB5353 (Delete/Error)

## Components
*   **Cards:** Material cards with 7dp elevation, white background, rounded corners (8dp).
*   **Buttons:** Material buttons, primary color, rounded corners (8dp).
*   **Icons:** Contained in circular colored backgrounds (e.g., green, purple, yellow, red circles).
*   **Inputs:** Outlined text fields.
